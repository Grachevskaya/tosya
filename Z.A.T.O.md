# Z.A.T.O. // I Love the World and Everything In It

***Z.A.T.O. // I Love the World and Everything In It*** is a freeware visual novel developed and published by [Ferry // Nopanamaman](https://zato.fandom.com/wiki/Ferry_//_Nopanamaman). It was released on Steam for Microsoft Windows, macOS, and Linux on 11 November 2025.
