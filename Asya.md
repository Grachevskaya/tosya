**Asya** is the protagonist in Ferry/nopanamaman's visual novel, *Z.A.T.O // I Love the World and Everything In It.*

== Appearance ==
Asya has short, messy brown hair with bangs that reach just below ear length. Her eyes are a deep gold color with yellow pupils. 

All her sprites have her wearing the female school uniform. Which consists of a long, dark brown dress with long sleeves and a white lace collar, trim, and cuffs. Over the dress, there is an black apron with a large pocket and a small pin on her right side, which is presumed to be a Komsomol pin.

== Background ==
Asya was raised in the city of Vorkuta-5, a closed city known as a Z.A.T.O. (Закры́тое администрати́вно-территориа́льное образова́ние (ЗАТО́) in Russian, where she lives and attends school.  

== Personality == 
Asya is a quiet girl who is seen as an outcast in her class. At the beginning, she struggles to form sentences without either trailing off at the end or stuttering. This is shown at the start of the game, when [Vadim](https://zato.fandom.com/wiki/Vadim_Garin) is talking and making fun of Asya, furthermore, when she first talks to [Marina](https://zato.fandom.com/wiki/Marina_Kaplan) and [Ira](https://zato.fandom.com/wiki/Ira_Grachevskaya), she did the same.

Throughout the events of Z.A.T.O,, she becomes more confident talking to Marina, and eventually Vadim.

Asya has a complex view on life. She has deep insecurities, believing herself unworthy of love, yet very selfless and kind, asserting that she loves the universe. (“The universe is too kind to an ungrateful brat like me.”) As an enduring mechanism, she has a habit of turning suffering into enjoyment, often thinking that things could be far worse. For example, if she feels she would be hurt by an unexpected accident, she would likely think to the universe along the lines of “Ah. Thank you. Thank you so much. You could have done so much worse than this.”

She can sometimes spiral out of control, at the hint of abandonment she becomes scared and even sad. She is paradoxically strong willed in any non-social situation, for example, even in the face of concept like nihilism, she doesn’t really seem phased. She has excellent understanding of philosophy, yet is also deeply involved in her strange fantasies. To the point where she can convince herself that reading a fairy tale book was extraordinary enlightening.

== Relationships ==

=== [Ira Grachevskaya](https://zato.fandom.com/wiki/Ira_Grachevskaya) ===
Ira has multiple times bailed Asya from bullying and being picked on, most notably when she threw a chair at Vadim, stopping him from tormenting Ira further. Asya has a complicated appreciation of Ira, preferring to see her as a friend but understanding how their "friendship" is mostly one sided. Ira plays a central role in Asya's life from the start of the plot to near the end.

=== [Vadim Garin](https://zato.fandom.com/wiki/Vadim_Garin) ===
Vadim is the main source of torment to Asya, effectively leading a her classmates to engage in various cruel "pranks" which Asya endures. He frequently copies Asya's homework. They have at one point hung out, along with Marina, which seemed to have no effect on his treatment towards Asya for the rest of the plot.

=== [Marina Kaplan](https://zato.fandom.com/wiki/Marina_Kaplan) ===
Marina is a friend of Asya, meeting at first to discuss the disappearance of Ira, slowly building a friendship together. Marina is responsible for revealing to Asya how she has been keeping the ill Ira in her apartment.

=== [Tosya](https://zato.fandom.com/wiki/Tosya) ===
It is not clear whether Tosya is a result of the illness, a manifestation of Asya's willpower, or a real human being. She is the close friend of Asya, until sometime before the beginning of the plot, Tosya disappears from Asya's life until her eventual return later in the story.

=== [Lev Kaplan](https://zato.fandom.com/wiki/Lev_Kaplan) ===
Lev is the brother and caretaker of Marina. Lev reveals to Asya the nature of the disease, and conducts a test to evaluate her. He is the one who hands the pills capable of suppressing the illness, which she declines to take but attempts to gift to her parents.

== Trivia ==

* Ferry has drawn a gender bent version of Asya, along with the rest of the major cast, in the sketchbook portion of the gallery accessible from in-game.
* Asya enjoys reading children books despite her age.
* Based on the context of her location and the pin on her uniform, Asya is part of the Comsomol, the youth branch of the communist party of the Soviet Union.
* She hinted to be the source of the signal detected around Vorkuta-5 at the end of the game, with her message "I love you" written in Russian Morse code being detectable in regions around the city.
* She likes the drink Duchess :)
